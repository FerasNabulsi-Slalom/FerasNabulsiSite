About

Great platforms are products too. They deserve research, craft and a clear point of view.

As a Platform Design Director, I sit where design, engineering and strategy meet. I help organizations turn scattered interfaces into connected ecosystems, and fragmented teams into aligned ones.

My work spans design systems, platform strategy, service design and design operations, always with an eye on the people who use what we build and the people who build it.
